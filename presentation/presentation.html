Background

I earned my French Baccalauréat in 2020 (ST2S : Health and Social Sciences), initially aiming for a career in sports education or medicine. Over the following years, I explored several paths: competitive basketball training, esports coaching (top 0.25% League of Legends player, coaching around 40 players on gameplay analysis and performance), hospitality, and temp work across different industries,experiences that sharpened my English, my adaptability, and my ability to perform under pressure.

In 2024, I was diagnosed with Asperger's (autism spectrum) and ADHD. Understanding this about myself reframed a lot of what I'd always done instinctively: hyperfocus on tasks until they're done right, a drive for efficiency, and a pattern-first way of thinking that turns out to map naturally onto how code and logic work.

In 2025, I moved to Toulouse to pursue a new direction and enrolled at ADRAR Formation for a Web & Web Mobile Developer program. Discovering HTML and CSS was the turning point : coding just clicked, in a way traditional coursework never had. Through the program, I found my real interest lies less in web development itself and more in software engineering and embedded systems, with a growing focus on AI.

I'm now working toward a university or accredited engineering school pathway, while completing additional certifications (CS50 – Introduction to Computer Science, and others) alongside my current training.
